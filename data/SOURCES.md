# Relay dataset sources

Documents are chosen to address the barriers identified in our proposal: unclear eligibility, documents and proof, appeals and next steps, fear and distrust, and inaccessible communication (email-only processes, limited internet, hard-to-read letters). Added November 2026; **still needs TA (client) approval** for sources beyond the four proposal articles.

**Text:** U.S. government pages and regulations are public domain, so the full text is included. News articles are copyrighted, so those files are short labeled summaries with a link to the original; replace a summary with the full text (for example via the US 6 scraper) and keep the same slug. Text marked `[Relay note: ...]` was written by the team to connect related pages.
