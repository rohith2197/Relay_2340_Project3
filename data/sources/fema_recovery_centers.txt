Disaster Recovery Centers: in-person help without email or internet
Source: FEMA Disaster Recovery Center Locator, https://egateway.fema.gov/ESF6/DRCLocator (reached from fema.gov/drc; accessed November 2026). U.S. government work.

FEMA Disaster Recovery Centers (DRCs) are accessible facilities and mobile offices you can visit to learn about FEMA and other disaster assistance programs and to ask questions about your case. DRCs are set up in convenient places after a disaster so they are easy to find. The DRC Locator at fema.gov/drc shows the hours, services, and locations of DRCs near you.

A Disaster Recovery Center may be able to help you:
- Apply for assistance.
- Learn more about disaster assistance programs.
- Learn the status of your FEMA application.
- Understand any letters you get from FEMA.
- Find housing and rental assistance information.
- Get answers to questions or resolve problems.
- Get referrals to agencies that may offer other assistance.
- Learn about Small Business Administration (SBA) programs.

[Relay note, combining FEMA's DRC Locator and "What's Next?" pages] Other ways to apply or check your status if you do not have email or reliable internet:
- By phone: FEMA Helpline 1-800-621-3362. You can apply and check your status by phone.
- In person: at a Disaster Recovery Center. Staff can also scan documents into your file and accept appeal documents.
- Online: DisasterAssistance.gov.
