Valencians physically, emotionally exhausted six months on from floods (summary)
Summary written by the Relay team of a Euronews article by Méabh Mc Mahon, published April 29, 2025: https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/04/29/valencians-physically-emotionally-exhausted-6-months-on-from-floods. This is a summary, not the original text.

Barriers: heavy paperwork; long recovery.

Setting: the Valencia region of Spain, six months after the October 2024 DANA floods, which killed 227 people according to the article.

Main points:
- Six months on, residents said they felt more drained than right after the floods. The mental strain felt stronger even as homes were renovated and businesses slowly reopened.
- Families were still dealing with heavy paperwork and long renovations. One father of two described moving house for the second time after losing his renovated home.
- The economic damage ran into the billions of euros, and the psychological effects on children were described as severe.
- Volunteers played a large role in the clean-up, and there were public protests against the regional government's handling of the disaster.

Why it matters: recovery after a flood takes many months. People are still exhausted and still facing paperwork long after the disaster, so aid information needs to be simple, clear and easy to find, and emotional support matters too.
