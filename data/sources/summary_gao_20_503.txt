GAO-20-503: Additional Actions Needed to Strengthen FEMA's Individuals and Households Program (summary)
Summary written by the Relay team of a U.S. Government Accountability Office report published September 30, 2020: https://www.gao.gov/products/GAO-20-503.

Barriers: unclear eligibility; appeals and next steps.

Scope: FEMA's Individuals and Households Program (IHP) for disasters from 2016 through 2018.

Main points:
- About 5.6 million people applied for IHP help in that period. About 4.4 million were referred for assistance, and about 2 million were found eligible.
- The most common reasons applicants were found ineligible were: the damage was judged insufficient, the applicant did not submit evidence supporting their disaster losses, or the applicant did not make contact with the FEMA inspector.
- An "ineligible" determination is not always final. Applicants can send more information or appeal.
- Survivors often did not understand the application process, why they may have been denied, or what their appeal options were. GAO recommended that FEMA make the process clearer and simpler.
- A March 2024 FEMA rule later removed the requirement for IHP applicants to apply for a Small Business Administration (SBA) loan before getting some kinds of help.

Why it matters: many denials come from missing documents or missed contact with FEMA, not from the person being unaffected. Responding to FEMA letters, staying in touch with the inspector, and sending supporting documents can make the difference.
