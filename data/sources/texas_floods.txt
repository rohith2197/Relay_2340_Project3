Texas Hill Country floods, July 2025: barriers to recovery aid.

Some undocumented families avoided applying for FEMA assistance out of fear of immigration enforcement. Many did not know that a household can still qualify when a child in the home is a U.S. citizen.

Three months right after the floods, only about 22% of applicants in Kerr County had been found eligible. FEMA required an email address, which was a barrier for older residents with limited internet access, and it was unclear whether applicants knew they could appeal a denial.
